# gbcomp-RLB-issues
documentation of issues and possible solutions with Respondus Lockdown Browser on your device. Resource made for and by GBC computer tech students.

## Welcome!
Unfortunately, if you're here, we probably aren't finding you in the best of states. You've probably run into an issue with [Respondus Lockdown Browser,](https://web.respondus.com/he/lockdownbrowser/) the custom browser made to create a "secure" testing environment for tests and exams. Use of this program is mandatory for online students in computer programming and technology courses at George Brown (hereby referred to as GB) who cannot or do not want to come to a GB testing centre for midterms/finals, and may be required for all students by certain professors for quizzes/tests during the semester. This program is known to cause issues with computers it's installed on, some of which have already been encountered by our 2026-27 group of first-year students at GB. And we want to help. We're fed up with this invasive program messing up computers we rely on for schooling, or having to go and fork out cash to get another device so it won't mess with the only devices we already have. At a time where computer components are skyrocketing in price with no return in sight, this is of great concern to us.

In this repository is a documentation of the issues members of our year have encountered, and the solutions they've found. These solutions are provided by fellow computer programming and technology program students at GBC, who are only beginnign their post-secondary education in these programs. We always encourage [contacting the GBC's IT department]() with these issues, as suggested by professors. This is only small guide on issues you may encounter and *possible* fixes for them. We (owner, organizers, and contributors to this repository) do not take any responsibility for usage of the solutions provided in this document; you are to use this at your own discretion and risk. If you are not confident with a solution or with your execution of it, do not use this and please contact IT.

We thank anyone who has contributed to this resource: for your time and effort, and your care for your fellow students. 

## Template for Cases
please copy the below Markdown and use as a template to format your issue and solution.

```
# Issue [number]: [name]
encountered by [github username] on [date]
[PC/laptop/iPad/etc] [any additional tech specs you deem necessary]

## Description
provide a decent but simple description of the issue you encountered. Remember that there may be students reading this with very little to no knowledge of computers. Expect the same amount of experience that the college professors expect out of first year students.

## Solution:
provide the solution you used to fix this. Provide any resources that may have assisted you, including help centre forums, advice from IT as you see fit, etc. PLEASE provide screenshots if you can.
```
